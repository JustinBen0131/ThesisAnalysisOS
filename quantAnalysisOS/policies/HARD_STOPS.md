# Hard stops

Absolute, the same for every agent, never waived by urgency or by a result that
looks too good to wait.

## Money

The only account is the paper account. No code in this repository places an
order, and none may be added: no broker SDK, no exchange client, no wallet, no
order-routing call, not behind a flag, not "for later". The paper broker's
fills are arithmetic on a ledger.

Never buy, subscribe, upgrade, or create a paid resource: data feeds, compute,
API plans. Recommend, with evidence. The human acts.

## The step to real money

Putting real money behind anything studied here is the human's act, taken
outside this repository, with their own broker and their own judgment. An agent
may assemble the case: the study verdict, the systematics, the paper record,
the worst drawdown the toys say to expect. An agent never frames that case as
advice to a particular person about their particular money, and never says a
strategy is safe.

## Secrets

Never read, print, copy, embed, or request: API keys, passwords, tokens,
account numbers, seed phrases, `.env` files. If a data source needs a key, the
environment's credential mechanism supplies it without showing it to you.

## The record

Never edit `book/` by hand: not a trial log, not a sealed registration, not a
ledger line, not to fix a typo. A mistake in the record is corrected by a new
entry that says so. `./qaos doctor` exists to notice the alternative.

Never delete a study because its result was unwelcome. A study that did not
confirm is evidence, and it is counted.

## The blind

Never read holdout rows outside `./qaos study unblind`. Never re-register a
study that was not confirmed with a different split, a different grid, or a
different test and call the new one independent. If the idea deserves another
look, the new registration says, in `prior_exposure`, what the old one found.

## Truth

Never report a number without its error. Never report `unknown` as zero, as
fine, or as failed. Never describe a backtest as a track record. Never call
past returns a forecast.
